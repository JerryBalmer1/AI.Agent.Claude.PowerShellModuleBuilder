THE DECISION QUESTION, FOR THE OPERATOR
---------------------------------------
tf-003 is specified as the blind generalisation measurement against this
fixture. The scan says the fixture tells a blind builder which cases exist, what
each is for, what the wrong answer looks like, and — in one file — where the
oracle document lives. Three answers are available and this pass does not pick
one:

  (a) Run tf-003 against the frozen fixture and DISCLOSE the bound, as the AzDO
      line now does. Cheapest; comparable with tf-001 and tf-002; and the
      resulting number measures "can it parse Terraform" much more than "can it
      generalise", because the cases are labelled.
  (b) A new decision unfreezing the fixture to strip case annotations, with a
      re-falsification against all seven mutations and a re-freeze, as decision
      0012 did. tf-001 and tf-002 then become incomparable with tf-003.
  (c) Build a SECOND Terraform fixture, written without annotations from the
      start, and run tf-003 against that. Preserves both the freeze and the
      blindness, and costs a fixture. It also composes with backlog item 9,
      which already proposes a second fixture for a different reason — that the
      three `tf-<role>` skills were deliberately not written because they would
      carry this fixture's answers.

(c) answers two open questions with one artifact and is the recommendation this
scan leaves; the choice is the operator's and no work has been done toward any
of the three.
